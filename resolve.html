Resolution FAQ
1. How long do payouts take?
Payouts are completely instant. As soon as you tap Finalize, the backend processes the routing and credits the winning team leaders' wallets immediately.
2. How are funds deducted?
Any Rupee (Cash) prizes are deducted from your "My Earnings" balance. Any Point (PTS) prizes are deducted from your "My Points" balance. You must have enough funds before finalizing.
3. What is a "Custom Payout"?
The Custom button allows you to manually reward teams outside of the standard 1st/2nd/3rd podium. You can also use it to add bonus cash or points on top of a podium prize.
4. Why is a specific match not showing here?
Matches disappear from this screen once they are successfully marked as "Completed", "Resolved", or "Cancelled" in the database.
5. Can I edit payouts after finalizing?
No. Once the match is resolved, funds are permanently moved to the players. Please double-check your assignments before confirming.
6. Do solo players receive clan payouts?
Yes. If a solo player enters without a clan, the system automatically detects them as the leader and routes the prize to their personal wallet.
7. Can I give both Points and Cash?
Absolutely! Tap "Custom" or "Edit" on a team to mix both Rupees and Points in the same payout.
8. What happens if I make a typo in the amount?
Review the "Total Deduct" bar at the bottom before submitting. If the numbers look too high, untoggle the team and re-enter the correct custom values.
9. Why is the Submit button red?
The button locks itself if you have insufficient wallet funds or if you have forgotten to assign a mandatory podium position (like 1st Place).
10. Who do I contact for database errors?
If a match freezes or fails to resolve, tap the WhatsApp Escalation button below to contact Admin Support instantly.
